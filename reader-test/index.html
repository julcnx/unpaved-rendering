Which roads are gravel or dirt?

This is about roads, not tracks or trails. Many main roads and village roads are gravel or dirt (in Africa, Australia, Asia, South America), yet most maps draw them exactly like asphalt. A gravel highway should still look like a highway, so these drawings keep each road's colour and width and add a mark for gravel or dirt.

This test, for maps made from OpenStreetMap, checks which drawings people understand at a glance. Five small maps, each drawn its own way, with no legend on purpose: tap the numbered roads you read as gravel or dirt. Go with your first impression: you cannot go back to a map.
