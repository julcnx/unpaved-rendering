# Key numbers

Generated by `stats/report.ts` from 54 countries (Antarctica excluded), 2026-11-12. Quote figures from here so the wiki page and posts stay consistent. Road classes: motorway, trunk, primary, secondary, tertiary, unclassified, residential (no tracks, no service roads, no links).

## Data

- Road length: 20.0 million km.
- Untagged (no `surface`, no known `tracktype`, assumed paved): 58.1% of km.
- Unpaved: 26.4% of all km (minimum), 63.1% of tagged km (upper bound).
- Off-road mark: 0.8% of all km, 3.1% of Unpaved km (162,692 km).
- Off-road evidence (`smoothness`, `tracktype` or `4wd_only`) on 5.9% of Unpaved km: the mark can only be decided there, so it stays optional (per country: [mark-coverage.md](mark-coverage.md)).
- `surface=unpaved` is 75.4% of Unpaved km; then `ground` 9.9%, `gravel` 5.3%, `dirt` 4.1%, `compacted` 3.5%.
- Motorways: 41 km Unpaved out of 128,861 km.

## Main roads

- Highest Unpaved share of trunk: Gabon 45%, DR Congo 43%, Cameroon 28%, Kenya 26%, Bolivia 21%.
- Highest Unpaved share of primary: Gabon 87%, Namibia 66%, DR Congo 65%, Bolivia 58%, Cameroon 54%, Mali 53%.
- Highest Unpaved share of secondary: Namibia 95%, Mali 89%, Gabon 87%, Cameroon 74%, Bolivia 73%, Senegal 68%.
- Tertiary more than half Unpaved in 18 of 54 countries; highest: Mali 94%, Namibia 90%, Bolivia 90%, Ivory Coast 89%, Senegal 88%, Mongolia 81%.
- Most Unpaved km on trunk, primary and secondary: Brazil 42,131 km, Namibia 28,611 km, DR Congo 27,468 km, Australia 26,972 km, Peru 20,437 km, Bolivia 13,710 km.

## Official figures (CIA World Factbook)

- 34 countries compared: official unpaved share inside the OSM range (all km to tagged km) in 15, higher than the OSM upper bound in 18, lower in 1. OSM shares are conservative.

## Street-level imagery

- 55 roads tagged unpaved checked on street-level imagery: 41 confirmed Unpaved, 9 paved now (16%), 5 mixed.
- Of the 9 paved now: 6 were paved after the unpaved tag was set, 3 were tagged unpaved after imagery already showed pavement.
- Untagged roads, Malaysia pilot: 16 of 16 visible were paved, but only 17 of 872 random untagged ways had imagery.
