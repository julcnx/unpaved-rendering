## Text

**Ever planned a route on a nice solid line and ended up on gravel?**

Whether you follow the app on the bars or plan at home and ride a GPX, it's the same problem: most map apps draw dirt and gravel exactly like tarmac.

And it's not bad data. OpenStreetMap mappers have tagged the surface, the apps just don't show it. Unpaved, at least:
🇧🇴 Bolivia: 58% of primary roads
🇳🇦 Namibia: 95% of secondary roads
🇰🇬 Kyrgyzstan: a third of primary roads
🇲🇳 Mongolia: 61% of secondary roads
🇮🇸 Iceland: 65% of secondary roads
🇦🇺 Australia: 45% of tertiary roads
Across 54 countries, at least a quarter of all road length.

I'm a regular contributor to OpenStreetMap and the open map tools built on it, so I wrote a proposal: Mind the Surface. Roads keep their usual colour, so you still see which one is the main road, and the unpaved stretches get a simple pattern like dashes. You'd see the gravel while planning, before you commit to the route. I'm now taking it to app makers one by one.

Two questions:
👉 Which app do you plan or ride with, and does it show you the gravel?
👉 Know someone who builds a map or navigation app? Please send this their way.

https://julcnx.github.io/unpaved-rendering

Follow me for more on this and other map topics.
