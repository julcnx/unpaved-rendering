## Text

**Ever followed a nice solid line on the map and ended up on gravel?**

It happened to me between Pai and Wiang Haeng in Thailand, and it's not bad data. OpenStreetMap mappers have tagged the surface: across 20 million km of roads in 54 countries, at least a quarter is unpaved. That's 87% of primary roads in Gabon and 95% of secondary roads in Namibia. Most map apps just draw it all like tarmac.

So I wrote a proposal called Mind the Surface. Roads keep their usual colour, so you still see which one is the main road, and the unpaved stretches get a simple pattern like dashes. I tested the design with a reader survey and reworked it, and I'm now taking it to app makers one by one, with data, mockups, a live demo and a review of about 30 apps.

Two questions:
👉 Which app do you ride with, and does it show you the gravel?
👉 Know someone who builds a map or navigation app? Please send this their way.
