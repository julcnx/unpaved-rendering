# Facebook post (Julien's wall)

Draft, 2026-11-28. Images in this folder, rendered by `node scripts/fb-images.mjs`, all 1080 px wide for phones. Upload in the numbered order: the first one is the cover.

## Text

**Mind the Surface 🗺️ Maps should show you where the pavement ends.**

Two weeks ago I posted about the Pai to Wiang Haeng road, which map apps draw as a confident solid line even though it's dirt and gravel. Your comments and stories went straight into what came next.

I've spent years mapping and contributing to map tools, and that post finally pushed me to write it all up. The numbers: across 20 million km of roads in 54 countries, at least a quarter is unpaved. That's 87% of primary roads in Gabon and 95% of secondary roads in Namibia. Mappers have already tagged it. Most apps just don't show it.

The fix: roads keep their usual colour, and the unpaved stretches get a simple pattern like dashes. I tested the design with a reader survey and reworked it based on the results.

The proposal comes with data, mockups, a live demo and a review of about 30 apps. I'm now taking it to app makers one by one.

If you know someone who builds a map or navigation app, please send this their way 👇

https://julcnx.github.io/unpaved-rendering
